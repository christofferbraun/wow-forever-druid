# Travel

[← Index](../README.md) · Prev: [Route](route.md) · Next: [Dungeons](dungeons.md)

Druids get the best low-level travel toolkit in the game, and as a Horde druid you get a free one-way teleport loop that nobody else has. Set it up early.

---

## Teleport: Moonglade — the free teleport

**How you get it:** at level 10 you take the druid class quest **Heeding the Call**. Its follow-up, **Moonglade**, teaches you **Teleport: Moonglade the moment you accept it** — you do not have to complete anything first. 🔶

You then travel to Moonglade and speak with **Dendrite Starblaze** in the village of **Nighthaven** to finish the chain. 🔶

**What it is:** an on-demand teleport to Moonglade, usable from anywhere, on a cooldown. It is effectively a second hearthstone that goes somewhere useful.

❓ **Verify:** whether you have already done this — you are level 12, so the quest should have been available two levels ago. If `Teleport: Moonglade` is not in your spellbook, go see your trainer in Thunder Bluff's Elder Rise.

---

## The free flight home — Bunthen Plainswind

This is the part you flagged, and it is correct:

> **In Nighthaven, near the center of town, is Bunthen Plainswind — the Thunder Bluff flight master. The flight from Moonglade to Thunder Bluff is free.** 🔶

So the loop is:

```
Anywhere in the world
   → Teleport: Moonglade      (instant-ish, free, on cooldown)
   → walk to Bunthen Plainswind in Nighthaven
   → free flight to Thunder Bluff
```

**The catch:** the flight takes almost **seven minutes**. 🔶 It is free, not fast.

### When the loop is actually worth it

| Situation | Use the loop? |
|---|---|
| Deep in Kalimdor, hearth on cooldown, need Thunder Bluff | **Yes.** This is exactly what it is for. |
| Stranded in the Eastern Kingdoms with no gold | **Yes** — Teleport: Moonglade is the cheapest way back to Kalimdor you own. Very relevant for the [Undercity RFC quest](dungeons.md#the-pickup-run). |
| You need to be in Thunder Bluff in under two minutes | **No.** Hearth, or pay for a normal flight. |
| Hearthstone is up and set to Thunder Bluff | **No.** Just hearth. |

**Alliance note, for completeness:** the equivalent NPC is Silva Fil'naveth (Rut'theran Village). Irrelevant to you, but the two sit side by side in Nighthaven and it is easy to click the wrong one.

### Use the seven minutes

The flight is unattended time. Queue it deliberately: start the flight, then go do something else. It is also the single best window in the game to read a quest log or plan a route.

## Hearthstone discipline

Your hearth is a real resource. Plan it around what you are doing.

| While you are… | Set hearth to |
|---|---|
| Finishing Mulgore (levels 1–10) | **Bloodhoof Village** |
| Levels 10–16, training constantly, grabbing RFC quests | **Thunder Bluff** |
| Grinding the Barrens hard, already trained up | **The Crossroads** |
| Running Ragefire Chasm | **Orgrimmar** (the entrance is inside the city) |
| Questing Ashenvale / Stonetalon | The local Horde hub (Splintertree, Sun Rock Retreat) |

**The key insight:** because you have `Teleport: Moonglade` as a second escape, you can afford to set your hearth somewhere aggressive — a quest hub rather than a capital city. Non-druids cannot. Use that.

## The Thunder Bluff lifts

Thunder Bluff is vertical and the lifts are slow. Two things worth knowing:

- The **flight master and the main services sit on the central rise**; Elder Rise (druid trainer, **Rahauro** for [RFC quests](dungeons.md#the-pickup-run)) is a separate rise and a separate lift.
- **You can jump down** from the rises to the ground with Bear Form's health buffer absorbing the fall — much faster than the lift when you are heading out of town. ❓ Verify fall damage at your level before you try it from the top.

## Flight paths to collect

Buy every path you walk past. Prices are trivial and you cannot buy them remotely.

**Priority for levels 12–20:**

| Path | Why |
|---|---|
| Thunder Bluff | Home. Also the Moonglade return. |
| **The Crossroads** | Your Barrens hub. |
| **Camp Taurajo** | Southern Barrens access. Grab it on the way down even if you do not quest there. |
| **Ratchet** | Docks, neutral AH, boat to Booty Bay later. |
| **Orgrimmar** | Required for [Ragefire Chasm](dungeons.md). Get this before you start the RFC pickup run. |
| Sun Rock Retreat (Stonetalon) | For the 15–20 stretch. |
| Splintertree Post (Ashenvale) | For the 18–25 stretch. |

**For the Undercity RFC quest** you need to cross to the Eastern Kingdoms: Orgrimmar → zeppelin tower → Undercity. Pick up the **Undercity** flight path while you are there, and the **Tirisfal** paths if you plan to run [Ruins of Lordaeron](dungeons.md#the-ladder) or Scarlet Monastery later.

**Make that one trip do four jobs:** Varimathras' RFC quest, the **[library book turn-in](gear.md#library-books--the-best-gear-per-effort-in-the-game)** with Owen Thadd in the Magic Quarter (73.4, 33.0), the Brill book in Tirisfal (59.4, 52.3), and the flight paths. Undercity is the highest-value detour in your level range.
