# Forever vs Classic

[← Index](index.md) · Next: [The Druid Kit](druid-kit.md)

Forever is built on Classic, so almost all your Classic muscle memory transfers. These are the changes that actually change *decisions* while leveling 1–30.

---

## 1. Dungeon XP is inverted: quests are the payday

This is the single most important rule change for you.

- Dungeon **mob kill XP is way down**. 🔶
- Dungeon **quest XP is much, much higher**. 🔶 A dev interview confirmed quest XP is meant to outweigh kills.

**What this means:** never walk into a dungeon without its quests. Clearing Ragefire Chasm without the quest log is close to a waste of an hour; clearing it with all six is a chunk of a level. This is why the [RFC pickup run](dungeon-quests.md#ragefire-chasm) is written out as a route rather than a list.

It also means dungeon spam is no longer a leveling strategy. One clear per dungeon, quests in hand, then back outside.

---

## 2. Respecs cost 1 silver during beta

From Blizzard's beta dev notes. This is the thing that makes your whole Resto question easy — you are not locked into anything right now. Experiment freely, and treat every build in [Talents](talents.md) as reversible for pocket change.

Assume this reverts to normal escalating costs at launch. Do your experimenting *now*.

---

## 3. Rested XP moved outdoors: campfires

- Placing a **Basic Campfire** creates a campsite; sit at it to accrue rested XP in the wild. 🔶
- You can **craft and cast while waiting** at a campfire ✅ (refined in the Sept 24 notes) — so it isn't dead time.
- Leatherworking's **Camp Tent**, and the Tanning Rack / Sewing Machine that replace it, are the versions that grant rested XP proper: ~5% of a level, once an hour. 🔶
- Inns and cities still bank rested XP the Classic way.

**What this means:** logging out at the Crossroads inn still matters, but you no longer have to walk back to an inn to bank a rest bonus mid-session.

---

## 4. Food is an XP buff

Most cooked dishes give **+5% XP from kills** while well fed. 🔶 Note: *kills only, not quests.* Given rule #1 above, this is a nice-to-have rather than a priority — but you're a Tauren in the Barrens drowning in meat, so cook it.

---

## 5. The Legacy system

Account-wide horizontal progression, new to Forever.

- You earn **Legacy Points** from challenges — level milestones, profession milestones, exploration.
- Points unlock **account-wide**, but **each character spends them independently**. Your druid's 16 points are hers alone even though the pool is shared.
- **16 points spendable per character** at launch; the account pool caps at **65**. Extra points past 16 still advance the account-wide reward track.
- Three trees: **Adventures**, **Professions**, **Resourcefulness**.

**Relevant perks for a first character:** faster rested XP accumulation, a raised rested XP cap (`Well Rested`), and reduced campfire cooldowns. On a single leveling character, the rested-XP perks are the ones that compound — take those first.

## 6. Druid changes

Full detail in [The Druid Kit](druid-kit.md) and [Talents](talents.md). The headline numbers:

| Change | Impact on you |
|---|---|
| **Wrath damage up ~50%** ✅ | Wrath is now a genuinely good filler, not a placeholder. Reshapes the solo opener. |
| **Thorns scales with spell power** ✅ | Thorns is damage now. Keep it up always; it's best while you're being hit, which rewards the bear phase. |
| **Rejuvenation can crit** ✅ | Your self-sustain HoT got noticeably stronger. See the [pre-HoT trick](rotations.md#the-core-trick-pre-hot-then-shift). |
| **Tranquility can crit** ✅ | Matters later, at 30+. |
| **Omen of Clarity & Nature's Grasp are now trained**, not talents 🔶 | You get free-cast procs and a root-on-attack without spending Balance points. Big for Resto. |
| **Every druid learns Revive** 🔶 | Out-of-combat res without a talent. |
| **Mangle → renamed Primal Bite**, signature debuff removed ✅ | Bear gets a real attack button. Feral-flavored, but you'll use bear while soloing. |
| **Lacerate added for Bear** 🔶 | More bear damage/threat over longer fights. |

Talent trees were reworked substantially: of 51 druid talents, **13 are new, 33 changed, 5 unchanged, and 8 Classic talents are gone entirely**. 🔶 New 31-point capstones for Feral (**Berserk**) and Restoration (**Wild Growth**); Balance keeps Moonkin Form and gains **Eclipse**.

## 7. Odds and ends

- **Spellcaster weapons grant spell damage from level 10.** 🔶 Check your staff/mace — stat-stick logic differs from Classic, so re-read greens you'd have vendored.
- The XP **curve itself is unchanged** 🔶 — overall pace is Classic-like. It's the *sources* that moved.
- New UI: **Cooldown Manager**, plus improved gamepad support ✅.
- New content you'll brush against: race **Skyborne**, zone **Zephras Isle** (1–12), zone **Riverglades** (mid-30s–40s, 150+ quests) ✅.
