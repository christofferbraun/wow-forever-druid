## The 21-point ceiling

The most important thing to know before you plan anything:

> **At level 30 you have 21 talent points, not 30.**

Talent points start at level 10 and you get one per level, so `points = level − 9`.

| Level | Points | What that reaches in Resto |
|---|---|---|
| 10 | 1 | first point |
| 16 | 7 | **Tier 2 opens** |
| 25 | 16 | **Swiftmend** (needs 15 spent) |
| **30** | **21** | **Nature's Swiftness** (needs 20 spent) ← your beta goal |
| 39 | 30 | — |
| **40** | **31** | **Wild Growth** (needs 30 spent) — the Resto capstone |

**So: Wild Growth is a level-40 talent, not a level-30 one.** "Full Resto by 30" tops out at Nature's Swiftness. 🔶

The good news is that this is a *clean* stopping point. Nature's Swiftness lands on exactly the level you are aiming for, and it is the single best button in the low-level Resto kit. Level 30 pure Resto is a real, complete build — it just is not the capstone build.

## The honest answer on solo Resto

**Resto-first works, and a full respec at 30 is almost certainly unnecessary.**

Three things make it work in Forever specifically:

1. **Respecs cost 1 silver during beta** ✅. The question is nearly moot right now. Flip builds as often as you like; it costs less than a stack of bread. Do your experimenting *before* launch, when it is free.
2. **Wrath got buffed ~50% and Thorns now scales with spell power** ✅. Resto's damage problem is materially smaller than it was in Classic. Your kill speed as a pure Resto druid in Forever is noticeably better than the Classic equivalent.
3. **There are damage talents inside the Resto tree.** `Naturalist` reduces Healing Touch cast time *and* boosts damage output 🔶. That is a solo-leveling talent that costs you nothing in healing progression — it is on the way to everything else.

**What Resto actually costs you:** kill speed. Not survivability, not sustain, not mana. You will kill mobs meaningfully slower than a Feral druid. What you get in exchange is that you almost never stop — rolling self-Rejuvenation means no drinking, no corpse runs, no downtime. Over an hour of play the gap is much smaller than it feels per-mob.

**The fix for kill speed is not talents, it is [rotation](rotations.md).** Thorns uptime, front-loaded dots, and bear-phase discipline buy you far more than 5 points in Improved Wrath would.

### The call

**Recommendation: go Resto the whole way. No respec needed at 30.**

Take the build below. It is pure Resto, it never needs correcting, and it arrives at Nature's Swiftness exactly at level 30.

**If kill speed frustrates you:** spend 5 points on `Improved Wrath` in Balance around level 12–20, then respec them out for 1 silver whenever you want. Zero risk. This is the "utility dip" you were asking about and it is completely safe under beta respec costs.

## The build: pure Resto, levels 10 to 30

Point order matters more than the endpoint. This order front-loads the things that help you solo.

### Levels 10-15 (points 1-6): Tier 1

| Points | Talent | Why here |
|---|---|---|
| 1–5 | **Naturalist** (5) | Shorter Healing Touch *and* more damage 🔶. The best possible first five points for a solo Resto leveler — it helps both halves of your job. |
| 6 | **Nature's Focus** (1/5) | Start it. Fewer interrupted casts while something chews on you. |

### Levels 16-20 (points 7-11): Tier 2 opens

| Points | Talent | Why here |
|---|---|---|
| 7–9 | **Gift of Nature** (3/5) | Straight healing effectiveness. Every heal you cast, forever. Highest-value healing talent in the tree. |
| 10–11 | **Natural Shapeshifter** (2/3) | −10% per rank on shift cost. You shift constantly while soloing; this is a mana talent disguised as a utility talent. |

### Levels 21-25 (points 12-16): to Swiftmend

| Points | Talent | Why here |
|---|---|---|
| 12–13 | **Gift of Nature** (5/5) | Finish it. |
| 14–15 | **Improved Rejuvenation** (2/3) | Rejuvenation is your most-cast spell both soloing and healing. It can crit now ✅. |
| **16** | **Swiftmend** (1) | Requires 15 spent. Instant heal off a HoT, and Forever made it heal for the HoT's *full duration* 🔶. Take it the moment you can. |

### Levels 26-30 (points 17-21): to Nature's Swiftness

| Points | Talent | Why here |
|---|---|---|
| 17 | **Improved Rejuvenation** (3/3) | Finish it. |
| 18–19 | **Tranquil Spirit** (2/5) | Cheaper Healing Touch. Filler with a real payoff in dungeons. |
| 20 | **Nature's Focus** (2/5) or **Tranquil Spirit** (3/5) | Filler to reach 20 spent. Either is fine — Nature's Focus if you are getting interrupted, Tranquil Spirit if you are running dry. |
| **21** | **Nature's Swiftness** (1) | Requires 20 spent. **This is your level-30 payoff.** Instant-cast any nature spell — pair it with Healing Touch for an instant full heal, or with Healing Touch on yourself to survive a bad solo pull. |

### Summary table

| Talent | Final rank at 30 |
|---|---|
| Naturalist | 5/5 |
| Nature's Focus | 1–2 /5 |
| Gift of Nature | 5/5 |
| Natural Shapeshifter | 2/3 |
| Improved Rejuvenation | 3/3 |
| Tranquil Spirit | 2–3 /5 |
| **Swiftmend** | 1/1 |
| **Nature's Swiftness** | 1/1 |
| | **= 21 points** |

## The optional Balance dip

If you want faster kills for the 12–25 stretch and do not mind a 1-silver correction later:

```
5 points → Improved Wrath  (Balance T1: reduced cast time and mana cost)
```

With Wrath already buffed ~50% ✅, five points of cast-time reduction on top is a real difference in kill speed. `Moonglow` (3 points, −mana on damage spells) is the alternative if mana is the problem rather than speed.

**The cost:** you reach Swiftmend at level 30 instead of 25, and Nature's Swiftness slips past 30 entirely. That is the tradeoff. Under 1-silver respecs it is reversible at any moment, so take the dip if the grind is annoying you and buy it back before you start healing dungeons seriously.

**Do not** dip Feral for the leveling stretch. See below.

## Talents to skip, and why

| Talent | Why skip |
|---|---|
| **Furor** | Forever rebuilt it: Cat Form now restores 20% of the energy you had when you last left Cat Form, +2/sec outside forms, capped at 20 🔶. That is a Cat-weaving talent, and you do not have Cat Form until 20 or a weaving rotation at all. Much weaker for a Resto leveler than the Classic version. |
| **Subtlety** | Threat reduction. Irrelevant solo, and in a 5-man at this level threat is not your problem. |
| **Reflection** | Mana regen while casting is good — but it competes with `Gift of Nature` and `Natural Shapeshifter` for tier-2 points and loses to both before 30. Revisit at 40+. |
| **Improved Regrowth** | Needs 25 points spent. You do not have the budget before 30. |
| **Living Spirit / Improved Tranquility** | Both need 20 spent, and both lose to Nature's Swiftness for your 21st point. |
| **Feral tier 1–2** | `Heart of the Wild` and `Thick Hide` are tempting for the bear phase, but bear is a *survival* tool for you, not a kill-speed tool — and it is already survivable. Points here delay Swiftmend and Nature's Swiftness for a benefit you do not need. |
| **Wild Growth** | The capstone, needs 30 spent = **level 40**. Not reachable in beta. Plan for it, do not plan around it. |

## Full Restoration tree reference

From Forever talent calculators — ❓ verify ranks and numbers in-game, these shift during beta.

**Tier 1**
- Nature's Focus (5) — 14% chance to avoid interruption from damage while casting Arcane/Nature spells
- Furor (5) — energy/rage on shapeshift (reworked, see above)
- Naturalist (5) — reduces Healing Touch cast time, boosts damage output
- Subtlety (3) — threat reduction on Nature/Arcane spells

**Tier 2**
- Natural Shapeshifter (3) — −10% mana cost per rank on all shapeshifting
- Reflection (3) — mana regeneration while casting
- Gift of Nature (5) — healing spell effectiveness
- Gift of the Earthmother (1) — global cooldown reduction on healing spells

**Tier 3**
- Tranquil Spirit (5) — mana cost reduction, Healing Touch / Tranquility
- Improved Rejuvenation (3) — Rejuvenation effect increase
- **Swiftmend** (1) — 15 points — instant heal consuming a HoT
- **Nature's Swiftness** (1) — 20 points — next Nature spell is instant

**Tier 4**
- Living Spirit (3) — 20 points — Spirit increase
- Improved Tranquility (2) — 20 points — threat reduction, shorter cooldown
- Improved Regrowth (5) — 25 points — Regrowth crit chance

**Capstone**
- **Wild Growth** (1) — 30 points — instant; heals the target and their party for 336 over 7 sec, party members within 43 yards of the target

### Balance and Feral tiers 1-2, for dip planning

**Balance T1:** Improved Wrath (5) · Genesis (5) · Moonglow (3) · Improved Moonfire (2)
**Balance T2:** Nature's Majesty (2) · Nature's Reach (2) · Improved Entangling Roots (3) · Nature's Splendor (1)

**Feral T1:** Ferocity (5) · Heart of the Wild (5) · Feral Swiftness (2) · Feral Instinct (3)
**Feral T2:** Brutal Impact (2) · Thick Hide (3) · Savage Fury (2) · Feral Charge (1, 10 pts)

Note that `Omen of Clarity` and `Nature's Grasp` are **trained, not talented** in Forever 🔶 — in Classic these were Balance talents. That is a quiet but significant buff to Resto leveling: you get both for free.
