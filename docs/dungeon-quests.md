# Dungeon Quests

[← Index](index.md) · Prev: [Dungeons](dungeons.md) · Next: [Gear and Loot](gear.md)

Every Horde-accessible dungeon from 1 to 30, in level order, with each quest's giver, turn-in, coordinates, and a copy-paste `/way` line.

> **`/way` needs an addon.** The command comes from **TomTom**, which adds a map arrow and waypoint queue. Paste a `/way` line into chat and it drops a waypoint. Without it the coordinates still work with any coordinate display addon.

> **Why this matters more in Forever:** dungeon kill XP is down and dungeon quest XP is up 🔶, so arriving without the quest log is the single most expensive mistake available. See [Forever vs Classic](forever-vs-classic.md#1-dungeon-xp-is-inverted-quests-are-the-payday).

Coordinates are community-sourced from Forever databases as of 2026-10-02 🔶. Verify before committing to a long ride.

## Scarlet Monastery

| | |
|---|---|
| **Recommended level** | 30 |
| **Level range** | 26-45 across four wings |
| **Entrance** | Tirisfal Glades — Northeastern corner<br>`/way Tirisfal Glades 85 30` |
| **Bosses** | Interrogator Vishas · Bloodmage Thalnos · Houndmaster Loksey · Arcanist Doan · Herod · Scarlet Commander Mograine · High Inquisitor Whitemane |
| **Quests** | 4 Horde quests in range, plus one at 33. Four wings - Graveyard, Library, Armory, Cathedral - at different levels. |

Your level-30 target. Four wings at different levels, so it stays useful well past 30. Graveyard is the lowest wing; Library holds Arcanist Doan, who drops the Illusionary Rod. Compendium of the Fallen is restricted to Orc, Tauren and Troll - you qualify.

**Gear:** Arcanist Doan (Library) drops Illusionary Rod: +44 spell power, +14 crit, +10 Sta, +7 Spi. That is a level 33-34 weapon and your next staff upgrade after Living Root. See [Gear](gear.md#dungeon-drops-to-watch-for).
